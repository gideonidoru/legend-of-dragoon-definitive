## Selective reuse assessment

1. **Action hints and controller presentation: highest priority.** [FooterActionsHud](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/combat/ui/FooterActionsHud.java) supplies contextual menu/battle actions and hideable colors. However, it hard-codes face-button pictures by semantic action and English labels; [ControllerStyleConfigEntry](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/modding/coremod/config/ControllerStyleConfigEntry.java) defaults to PlayStation, with optional autodetection. Our [InputCodepoints](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/blob/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/core/platform/input/InputCodepoints.java) already handles controller families and derives action labels from actual gamepad/keyboard bindings. Adapt the footer design using that system, localization, and reconnect/remapping checks; copying pictures wholesale could mislabel remapped controls.

2. **Inventory workflow: useful incremental work.** [EquipmentScreen](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/inventory/screens/EquipmentScreen.java) cycles alphabetical/power/icon sorts and slot filters. [ShopScreen](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/inventory/screens/ShopScreen.java) uses quantity confirmations for purchases/sales. Our engine already has [ItemStack](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/blob/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/game/inventory/ItemStack.java), inventory capacity configuration, and [campaign stack-size configuration defaulting to one](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/blob/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/lodmod/config/ItemStackSizeConfig.java). Reuse grouping/filter/quantity interaction ideas without importing older inventory storage. Verify full bags, gold limits, protected equipment, overflow, and cancel behavior.

3. **Addition training feedback: useful but separate from rule changes.** [AdditionOverlaysEffect44](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/combat/effects/AdditionOverlaysEffect44.java) distinguishes early/late/wrong/perfect inputs. Our upstream already provides automatic Additions, timing-window adjustment, overlay controls and [AdditionHitEvent](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/blob/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/game/modding/events/characters/AdditionHitEvent.java); this event alone lacks failed-input timing details. Assess a small diagnostic seam for training rather than replacing the combat overlay. The fork's [AdditionConfigs](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/combat/AdditionConfigs.java) changes per-hit frame data; flawless rewards, misinput tolerance, counter frequency, frenzy and difficulty alter gameplay. Keep those outside faithful mode. Turn order also [already exists upstream](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/tree/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/turnorder).

## Persistence risks and engineering inference

The fork's [GameEngine](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/core/GameEngine.java) writes/reads through V7; [our baseline writes V10](https://github.com/Legend-of-Dragoon-Modding/Severed-Chains/blob/fba1543543865e29ee572f479003d9b47158eeb3/src/main/java/legend/core/GameEngine.java). Its registered readers do not support V10: current saves cannot simply be opened there. [Statistics](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/statistics/Statistics.java) adds per-save `.stats` sidecars, also feeding bestiary progress. Saves and sidecars are written separately; statistics IO errors are logged and swallowed. Backups/migration would need both files and explicit consistency rules. Older saves cannot reconstruct unrecorded history.

[Ironman GameOver](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/title/GameOver.java) deletes the entire campaign directory, stronger than the release's save-file wording. Exclude this behavior. [Battle mastery updates](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/combat/Battle.java#L3955) award two mastery points for flawless Additions instead of one; [PhysicalDamageFormula](https://github.com/FrancisDionne/Severed-Chains/blob/35a7b14616d038d9616a6012f4c44002379cf914/src/main/java/legend/game/combat/formula/PhysicalDamageFormula.java#L38) also adds damage. Inference: disabling those settings later cannot restore earlier retail progression. Adopt presentation improvements independently, with optional gameplay settings and explicit save contracts.
